Applicable Regulatory & Reference Sources

  • 14 CFR §107.19 — Remote pilot in command responsibilities; defines ultimate authority and duties of the RPIC
  • 14 CFR §107.31 — Visual line of sight aircraft operation; underpins the visual observer's role
  • 14 CFR §107.33 — Visual observer requirements and communication duties with the RPIC
  • AIM (Aeronautical Information Manual), Ch. 4 — Standard radio phraseology and communication procedures
  • FAA-H-8083-25, Ch. 13 — Airport operations, traffic pattern communication, and CTAF procedures

Knowledge Prerequisites (before this deck)

  • Completion of Lesson 5 (Loading, Performance & Emergency Procedures) — emergency communication ties in here
  • Basic familiarity with airport traffic pattern terminology (upwind, crosswind, downwind, base, final)
  • Understanding of the difference between towered and non-towered (uncontrolled) airports

Required Lesson 6 Content Covered

  • Crew roles: PIC, visual observer, manipulator of controls
  • Communication and coordination best practices (CRM)
  • Standard aviation phraseology
  • Radio communication procedures at towered/non-towered airports
