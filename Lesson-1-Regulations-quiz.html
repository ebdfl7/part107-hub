Applicable CFR / Regulatory References

  • 14 CFR Part 107, Subpart A (General) — §107.1 Applicability, §107.3 Definitions
  • 14 CFR Part 107, Subpart B (Operating Rules) — §107.15 preflight condition, §107.21 in-flight emergency, §107.25 moving vehicle, §107.29 daylight/night operation, §107.31 VLOS, §107.33 visual observer, §107.35 multiple aircraft, §107.36 hazmat, §107.37 right-of-way, §107.39 operation over people, §107.41 operation in certain airspace, §107.51 operating limitations
  • 14 CFR Part 107, Subpart C — §107.61 certificate eligibility, §107.65 recurrent training
  • 14 CFR Part 107, Subpart D (Operations Over People) — §107.110–§107.145, Categories 1–4
  • 14 CFR Part 107, Subpart E (Waivers) — §107.200 certificate of waiver, §107.205 list of waivable sections
  • 14 CFR Part 89 — Remote Identification of Unmanned Aircraft (Standard Remote ID, broadcast modules, FRIAs)
  • 14 CFR Part 107.9 — Accident reporting requirements

Knowledge Prerequisites (before this quiz)

  • Comfortable with basic aviation terms: AGL, MSL, ATC, VLOS, RPIC
  • Have read 14 CFR Part 107 Subparts A and B at least once (Lesson 1 required reading)
  • No prior FAA certificate or flight experience required

Required Lesson 1 Content Covered

  • Who needs a Remote Pilot Certificate and when
  • Operating rules: altitude, speed, visibility, cloud clearance
  • Waivers and airspace authorization (LAANC)
  • Remote ID requirements: Standard Remote ID, broadcast modules, FRIAs
  • Night operations requirements (anti-collision lighting, no separate waiver since 2021)
  • Operations over people and moving vehicles (Categories 1–4)
  • Certificate privileges and recurrent training
  • Accident reporting requirements
