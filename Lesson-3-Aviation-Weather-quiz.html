Part 107 Lesson 3 — Aviation Weather Practice Quiz

questions covering METAR/TAF decoding, weather sources and briefings, wind/visibility/density altitude basics, and hazardous weather phenomena. One question at a time. Every item cites its source. Your weakest categories are tracked after each run.

Applicable Regulatory & Reference Sources

  • FAA Aviation Weather Handbook (FAA-H-8083-28) — METAR/TAF format, weather theory, hazardous phenomena
  • Aviation Weather Center — aviationweather.gov, the primary source for current METARs, TAFs, and weather charts
  • Leidos Flight Service — 1800wxbrief.com or 1-800-WX-BRIEF, for official pilot weather briefings
  • 14 CFR §107.51(c)–(d) — Minimum flight visibility and cloud clearance requirements

Knowledge Prerequisites (before this quiz)

  • Completion of Lessons 1–2 (Regulations, Airspace) is helpful but not required
  • Basic familiarity with UTC/Zulu time and statute miles
  • No prior meteorology background required

Required Lesson 3 Content Covered

  • Reading and decoding METAR codes
  • Reading and decoding TAF codes
  • Weather sources: Aviation Weather Center, FAA/Leidos weather briefings, PIREPs, SIGMETs
  • Wind, visibility, and density altitude basics
  • Microbursts, thermals, and wind shear (conceptual, non-powered-flight relevant)
