Applicable Regulatory & Reference Sources

  • 14 CFR §107.41 — Operation in certain (controlled) airspace; authorization requirement
  • 14 CFR Part 91, Subpart B — General flight rules, airspace operating rules
  • 14 CFR §91.137 — Temporary flight restrictions in the vicinity of disaster/hazard areas
  • FAA Aeronautical Chart User's Guide — Airspace symbology, colors, and line styles on sectional charts
  • FAA B4UFLY app / UAS Data Delivery System — Current airspace and TFR information source

Knowledge Prerequisites (before this quiz)

  • Completion of Lesson 1 (Regulations) — this lesson builds on §107.41 authorization requirements
  • Ability to identify basic map/chart features (scale, legend, north orientation)
  • Familiarity with AGL vs. MSL altitude references

Required Lesson 2 Content Covered

  • Controlled vs. uncontrolled airspace
  • Class B, C, D, E, and G identification and requirements
  • Special use airspace: restricted, prohibited, MOAs, warning and alert areas
  • Temporary Flight Restrictions (TFRs)
  • Sectional chart symbology: line colors, styles, and altitude figures
