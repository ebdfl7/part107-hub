Next Steps: Get Certified

Once you're working through lessons and passing practice tests consistently, here's how to actually get your Remote Pilot Certificate.

STEP 1 Get your FTN (FAA Tracking Number)

Create a free account at IACRA — select "Applicant," agree to the terms, and fill in your info. Your FTN appears on your account home screen. Write it down; you'll need it to schedule the test.

CREATE IACRA ACCOUNT ▸
STEP 2 Find a test center & schedule

The Part 107 knowledge test ("Unmanned Aircraft General – Small," UAG) is given at PSI-authorized centers, not FAA offices. Create a PSI account, search centers near you by zip code, and book a seat. Make sure your name matches exactly across your FTN, PSI account, and photo ID.

FIND A TEST CENTER ▸
STEP 3 Test day

Bring a valid government-issued photo ID. The exam is 60 questions, 2 hours, $175 (paid to PSI).

STEP 4 After passing

Log back into IACRA, click "Register," fill out the application (leave "Certificate Information" blank), and submit. You'll get a temporary certificate to print immediately — the permanent one arrives by mail.
