Applicable Regulatory & Reference Sources

  • 14 CFR §107.15 — Condition for safe operation; loading and performance planning tie directly to this requirement
  • 14 CFR §107.21 — In-flight emergency; RPIC may deviate from any rule of Part 107 to the extent required by the emergency
  • 14 CFR §107.9 — Accident reporting requirements if an emergency results in qualifying injury or property damage
  • Aircraft manufacturer flight manual / POH — Max takeoff weight, CG limits, and performance charts (aircraft-specific)

Knowledge Prerequisites (before this deck)

  • Completion of Lesson 4 (Weather Effects on UAS Performance) — density altitude and wind concepts carry over here
  • Familiarity with your specific aircraft's max takeoff weight and any published CG limits
  • Basic understanding of GPS-assisted vs. manual/attitude flight modes

Required Lesson 5 Content Covered

  • Loading limits and center of gravity (CG) considerations
  • Performance charts and aircraft-specific limits
  • Emergency procedures: lost link
  • Emergency procedures: GPS loss
  • Emergency procedures: low battery and flyaway
