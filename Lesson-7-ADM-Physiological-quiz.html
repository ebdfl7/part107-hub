Applicable Regulatory & Reference Sources

  • 14 CFR §107.27 — Alcohol or drugs; requires compliance with §§91.17 and 91.19
  • 14 CFR §91.17 — Alcohol: 8-hour "bottle to throttle" rule, 0.04% BAC limit, prohibition on operating while under the influence
  • 14 CFR §91.19 — Drugs: prohibition on operating while using a drug that affects mental or physical capabilities
  • 14 CFR §107.57 — Offenses involving alcohol or drugs; grounds for denial, suspension, or revocation of a remote pilot certificate
  • FAA Risk Management Handbook (FAA-H-8083-2) — ADM models: DECIDE, PAVE, IMSAFE, and risk management framework

Knowledge Prerequisites (before this quiz)

  • Willingness to honestly self-assess personal fitness to fly using models like IMSAFE

Required Lesson 7 Content Covered

  • ADM models: DECIDE, PAVE, and IMSAFE
  • Effects of drugs and alcohol on pilot performance
  • Effects of fatigue and stress on pilot performance
  • Risk management framework
